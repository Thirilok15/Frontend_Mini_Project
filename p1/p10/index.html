Exploring the Universe

Space exploration is the discovery and study of outer space using rockets, satellites, telescopes, and spacecraft. Scientists explore planets, stars, and galaxies to understand the origin of the universe and search for life beyond Earth.

Space Facts

  • Earth is the only known planet with life.
  • The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.
  • Jupiter is the largest planet.
  • The ISS orbits Earth every 90 minutes.
  • Light from distant stars takes years to reach us.

Recent Space Missions

Modern space agencies have sent rovers to Mars, launched powerful telescopes, and developed reusable rockets. These achievements help scientists learn more about our solar system and distant galaxies.
